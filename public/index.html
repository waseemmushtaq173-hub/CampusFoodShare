About CampusFoodShare

Every day campus mess halls, canteens and events throw away good cooked food while people nearby go hungry. CampusFoodShare gives donors a fast way to list surplus, ranks it by distance and time left, and lets NGOs and volunteers claim and collect it before it spoils, with a food-safety checklist and a PIN-verified handover.

How a handover works

  1. An NGO claims your listing and a 4-digit PIN is generated.
  2. You see the PIN under My Listings and share it with the volunteer at pickup.
  3. The volunteer enters the PIN to confirm collection.

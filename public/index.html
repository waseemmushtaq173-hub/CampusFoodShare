Rescue surplus campus food before it spoils.

CampusFoodShare connects hostel messes, canteens and events with nearby NGOs and volunteers in real time, ranked by distance and time-to-expiry, with a food-safety checklist and a verified handover PIN on every pickup.

About one-third of all food is wasted while millions of people go to bed hungry. Every plate rescued on campus is a warm meal for a child, a mother or an elder who needs it tonight.

How a handover works

  1. An NGO claims your listing and a 4-digit handover PIN is generated.
  2. You see the PIN under My Listings and share it with the volunteer at pickup.
  3. The volunteer enters the PIN to confirm collection, so every rescue is verified.

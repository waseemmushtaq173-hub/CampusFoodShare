GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to donate food?

No. Use the donation form on this page. You only need a name, a phone number and the pickup details. You will get a private tracking link to follow your donation. An account is only needed if you want a dashboard with your full history.

What food can I share?

Fresh, safely stored food: cooked meals, bakery items, fruit and vegetables or sealed packaged food. Please set a realistic pickup deadline (within 24 hours) and mention allergens in the notes. Do not share food that smells off, has been left uncovered or was cooked long ago.

What is the handover PIN?

When an NGO or volunteer claims your food, a 4-digit PIN is created. You see it on your tracking page. Give it to the volunteer when they arrive. They enter it to confirm the pickup, so only the right person collects your food.

Is my phone number safe?

It is shown on your listing so a team can call you about the pickup. We never sell it or use it for marketing. You can withdraw your listing at any time before it is claimed.

What if nobody claims my food?

Listings are ranked by distance and time left, so urgent food is shown first. If the deadline passes, the listing expires automatically. For large amounts, use the contact form and we will help find a team.

Who can claim the food?

Registered NGOs and volunteers. Anyone can create a free partner account.

How one donation travels through the system

Frontend, backend and database, in order.

  1. 1. Donor fills the formWebsite asks GET /api/match and shows suitable ashrams.
  2. 2. Backend validatesPOST /api/public/donations checks phone, safety, deadline, food type and spam.
  3. 3. Database stores itA listing and a private tracking token are saved. The donor gets a link.
  4. 4. Everyone is updatedServer-sent events refresh every open screen. NGOs see it ranked by distance and expiry.
  5. 5. NGO claims itA 4-digit PIN is generated. Food directed to an ashram is reserved for it for 30 minutes.
  6. 6. Verified handoverVolunteer enters the PIN, food is marked collected and counted in the impact numbers.
